## SemDH 2024 - The First International Workshop for Semantic Digital Humanities 

We held the first version of the [SemDH workshop](https://semdh.github.io) on May 27, 2024. It took place in Hersonissos, Crete (Greece) and was co-located with [ESWC 2024](https://2024.eswc-conferences.org/). 
The program contained a variety of full-paper presentations and a poster session for all accepted short papers. Out of 16 submissions, we were able to accept 6 full papers and 6 short papers. The keynote was given by [Marieke van Erp](https://mariekevanerp.com/) (Digital Humanities Lab, KNAW Humanities Cluster Netherlands) on "Knowing what you don't know - on gaps, quality issues and other challenges in DH use cases". The panel discussion provided a platform for sharing and discussing challenges from the diverse and interdisciplinary viewpoints from the Semantic Web, Cultural Heritage, and Digital Humanities communities.
